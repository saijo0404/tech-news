# How Gemini Flash agents are helping a Michigan dairy farmer

- **來源**: Google Blog
- **發布日期**: 2026-07-28
- **原文連結**: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/using-gemini-to-manage-farm/

## 核心主題
Michigan dairy farmer Paul Windemuller uses Gemini 3.6 Flash AI agents to automate farm data analysis and management, transforming his daily operations.

## 關鍵重點
- Paul built a local multi-agent AI system using Gemini 3.6 Flash to integrate siloed farm data from various sources like milk quality sensors, feed logs, and weather stations
- The system uses a directory-based file-interface instead of APIs or web scraping, keeping data under Paul's control and eliminating manual spreadsheet work
- The system calculates Daily Static Variable Margin (SVM) to optimize farm operations by isolating true biological and operational efficiency from market noise
- Gemini 3.6 Flash offers cost-effective AI agents with 17% reduction in output tokens compared to Gemini 3.5 Flash, making it accessible for small businesses

## 結論
By delegating office work to AI agents, Paul can reclaim his time to focus on actual cow management, demonstrating how cost-effective AI tools can help independent farmers streamline operations and scale their businesses beyond traditional spreadsheet limitations.
